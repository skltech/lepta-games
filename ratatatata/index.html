RATATATATA: RPG Shooter

Idle merge guns & boss raids. A mobile game for iPhone and Android by LEPTA SIA.

The world is a dangerous place, especially when evil spirits are always lurking, ready to devour you. But the yōkai overlooked one little thing: they can be fought. All you need is courage, agility, a sharp mind and a seriously big gun.

In RATATATATA you lead a squad of brave tanuki and protect the world from evil spirits in a stylish idle game.

What you do in the game

  • Lock and load. Collect and upgrade weapons, from simple crossbows to laser blasters and missiles.
  • Merge. Combine sushi and rolls of the same grade to make stronger ones.
  • Dress to impress. Suit up your squad in outfits and raise pets that fight with you.
  • Idle progress. Your tanuki keep hunting while you are away; come back to claim the rewards.
  • Rebirth. Hit a wall, rebirth and power up again.
  • Bosses and raids. Beat bosses, take on weekly raid bosses and compare your results on the leaderboards.
  • Explore. Forests, ancient ruins, mysterious lands and scorching deserts, all overrun by yōkai.

The game is free to play. It shows optional rewarded ads and some interstitial ads, and offers optional in-app purchases, including one that removes forced ads.

Sign-in and leaderboards

RATATATATA signs you in with Google Play Games on Android and Apple Game Center on iPhone. Sign-in creates your game account on our server, so your nickname and best results can appear on the in-game leaderboards and in weekly raids.

With Google sign-in the game only uses your Play Games player ID and gamer name. It does not read your e-mail, contacts or files and does not post anything on your behalf. You can play without signing in; the leaderboards then stay unavailable.

How we handle your data is described in the RATATATATA Privacy Policy.

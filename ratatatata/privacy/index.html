RATATATATA Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026. Applies to the mobile game RATATATATA: RPG Shooter for iPhone and Android (the "Game").

In short. The Game keeps your progress on your device. If you sign in with Google Play Games or Apple Game Center, our server keeps a game account with your player ID, nickname and leaderboard results. We collect device and gameplay data to run, balance and protect the Game, we use AppLovin MAX (with Google AdMob) for ads, and AppsFlyer and Meta to measure where installs come from. Personalised advertising and tracking only happen if you allow them in the consent prompts. You can ask us at any time to see or delete your data.

1. Who we are

The Game is developed and published by LEPTA SIA, a company registered in Latvia (European Union). LEPTA SIA is the controller of the personal data described here.

Privacy questions and requests: nik@skl.vc. Game support: support@lepta.games.

2. What data we collect

2.1 Data stored only on your device

Your game progress (stages, weapons, pets, outfits, currencies, settings) is saved on your device. Local reminders that the Game schedules are created on the device; we do not receive a push token.

2.2 Game account (only if you sign in)

  • Google Play Games (Android): your Play Games player ID and gamer name. The Game asks Google for a one-time code that our server exchanges for this profile. We do not receive your e-mail address, contacts or files.
  • Apple Game Center (iPhone): your Game Center player ID and the signature Apple provides to prove it.
  • Our account data: an internal player ID, the nickname you choose (shown publicly on the leaderboards), your leaderboard scores and raid results, and sign-in tokens.

2.3 Device, install and usage data

  • Device and app: device model, operating system and version, app version, device language and region settings, time zone, screen size, memory and processor class, network type.
  • Identifiers: a random install ID created by the Game, your player ID if signed in, the AppsFlyer ID, and, only if you allow it, your device's advertising ID (IDFA on iPhone).
  • IP address and country: our server records the IP address a request comes from and the country derived from it (with the DB-IP IP to Country Lite database). We do not use GPS or precise location.
  • Gameplay events: sessions (start, pause, end, duration), progress and game economy events, ad views and in-app purchase events. These events contain game values only, not names, e-mails or free text.
  • Install attribution: whether the install came from an ad campaign, the media source and the campaign name, as reported by AppsFlyer.
  • Integrity checks: on Android a Google Play Integrity verdict, on iPhone the App Store's signed app transaction, which tell us whether the Game is a genuine copy installed from the store. We use the result to keep the leaderboards fair.

2.4 Purchase data

When you buy something in the Game, the App Store or Google Play processes the payment. We receive the transaction ID, the product and the receipt or purchase token to check the purchase and deliver it. We never receive your card or bank details.

2.5 Data collected by partners

The ad, measurement and platform partners in section 4 collect data through their software in the Game, as described there and in their own privacy policies.

5. Ads, tracking and your choices

  • Consent in the Game. At first launch the Game shows the consent flow of AppLovin MAX: on iPhone Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland Google's consent message. If you do not consent, ads are not personalised, your advertising ID is not shared, and the Meta SDK sends no events.
  • AppsFlyer. On iPhone AppsFlyer uses the IDFA only if you allowed tracking. On Android the Game turns off advertising identifiers for AppsFlyer.
  • "Do not sell". The Game tells AppLovin MAX not to "sell" personal data for every player, in the sense of US state privacy laws.
  • Change your mind. iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Android: Settings > Google > Ads (reset or delete the advertising ID). You can also write to us to withdraw consent.
  • Remove forced ads. An in-app purchase turns off the ads that appear without you choosing them; rewarded ads stay optional.

6. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google under their terms and privacy policies. We keep a record of each purchase (transaction ID, product, player ID) to deliver it, prevent duplicates and handle refunds.

7. International transfers

Our server and analytics data are stored in the EU (Frankfurt). Some partners, such as AppLovin, AppsFlyer, Meta, Google, Apple and Unity, may process data in the United States or other countries. Such transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the partner is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. How long we keep data

  • Gameplay analytics events, with their IP address and country: deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Game account, nickname and leaderboard scores: until you ask us to delete them or we close the Game. Weekly raid results expire 120 days after the raid.
  • Sign-in tokens: up to 90 days after their last use.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to deliver purchases and handle refunds, and as long as accounting and tax law requires.
  • Data on your device: until you delete the Game.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR in the EU and UK and under US state laws), you can ask us to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. To do so, write to nik@skl.vc. We reply within one month. We may ask for details that prove the account is yours.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. Our lead authority is the Data State Inspectorate of Latvia (Datu valsts inspekcija, dvi.gov.lv); you can also contact the authority in your own country.

10. Deleting your data

To delete your RATATATATA account and data, e-mail nik@skl.vc with the subject "RATATATATA data deletion". Please include your nickname from the Game and whether you play on Android (Google Play Games) or iPhone (Game Center).

We then delete your game account, nickname, leaderboard entries and the analytics events linked to your player ID and install ID within 30 days. Purchase records that accounting or tax law requires us to keep are kept for that period only. Data on your device is removed when you delete the Game. Data collected by our partners can also be deleted through their own tools (see section 4).

11. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal data, write to nik@skl.vc and we will delete it.

12. Security

Data travels to our server over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are short-lived, refresh tokens are stored only as hashes, and access to our systems is limited to the people who run the Game.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game gets new features or partners. The current version is always on this page with its effective date. If a change is material, we will also tell you in the Game.
